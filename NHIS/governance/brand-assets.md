# Brand assets

- `assets/brand/Logo_NASTRA.svg` is the authoritative NASTRA logo. `assets/brand/Logo_NASTRA.png` (1243 × 1269) is its raster fallback. Both are byte-identical copies of the supplied files (SHA-256 recorded in `validation/verification-log.md`).
- The artwork is a white maple leaf with a white star and a red orbital ring (`#F13131` inside the SVG).
- Do not redraw, recolour, crop, outline, simplify or "improve" it.
- The leaf and star are white. Place the logo only on dark surfaces (`color.brand.logoBackground`, the canvas or panel). On a light ground it disappears.
- The red ring is part of the artwork only. It is not an interface token and never appears as a brand accent, highlight, selection, chart series, glow or call to action.
- Near a Warning or Emergency, the alert must be unmistakable from its label, glyph and hierarchy, never from red alone.
- Do not use the NASA insignia, logotype, seal or identifiers. Do not write "NASA approved", "official NASA" or anything that implies endorsement.
