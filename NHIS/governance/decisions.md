# Design decisions

Decisions NHIS made inside the room the higher sources leave open. Each one is labelled honestly: a project decision is never presented as a NASA requirement. Status follows the NASA usage guide vocabulary: `proposed`, `not evaluated`, `verified`, `conflict open`.

Source types: `project-override` (P0, product owner), `nhis-derived` (derived from NHIS rules), `design-decision` (P8 choice where higher sources are silent).

| ID | Decision | Type | Basis | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NHIS-IMPL-001 | Build Station components directly on React Aria Components. The Untitled UI starter kit is not used as the component layer; it may later serve only as an app shell. | project-override | Product owner confirmation, 2026-11-14; 03 §6, §22, §24 | proposed |
| NHIS-THEME-001 | One dark operational Station theme. No light theme. Grayscale and monochrome-green are review modes, not themes. | project-override | Product owner choice; 03 §23 ("add a theme only if it serves an environment or testing need"). Rationale: shared visual language with EVA (black renders transparent on additive optics) and lower emitted luminance in a small cabin. The luminance rationale is a design judgement, not a sourced requirement. | proposed |
| NHIS-TYPE-001 | Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (UI) and Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono (numeric data). SIL OFL 1.1 via Fontsource. | project-override | Product owner choice; NHIS A22 (sans, fixed-width numerics, I/l/1 and O/0 distinct). Verified visually: slashed zero, tailed l, serifed I in Mono. | proposed |
| NHIS-TYPE-002 | 16 px (12 pt) minimum for labels, units, data age and alert fields. 14 px only for tertiary metadata and chart ticks. Nothing below 14 px. | nhis-derived | NHIS A21 (12 pt important content, 9 pt floor). Physical angular size depends on display PPI and distance: see U-08. | not evaluated |
| NHIS-COLOR-001 | Interaction is achromatic. Primary action is a light fill; selection and focus use luminance, weight and shape. | nhis-derived | NHIS A1 (no non-alert red/yellow), A28 (≤ 6 coding colours), Few 2007 via 01 | proposed |
| NHIS-COLOR-002 | Safety red is never a text colour. It is a fill behind light text or a stroke/shape. Caution yellow is a fill behind dark text or a stroke. | nhis-derived | NHIS A28 (avoid red on black), B22 (no red text in EVA). Applied to Station for one consistent rule. | proposed |
| NHIS-COLOR-003 | Hex values for advisory blue, caution yellow and warning red are NHIS choices tuned for contrast: fills carry 6:1 label text, strokes ≥ 3:1 on every surface. | design-decision | NASA App. F assigns red/yellow/blue; Tables F.5-1/F.5-2 not read (U-04). Contrast verified by `scripts/check-contrast.mjs`. | proposed |
| NHIS-COLOR-004 | Caution tint is 12% over panel; the others are 16%. | design-decision | Yellow is far lighter; equal alpha made Caution banners heavier than Warning. | proposed |
| NHIS-MODE-001 | Violet is the single non-alert coding colour, used only for simulation, training and what-if. Shown as header label, workspace frame and diagonal texture. | nhis-derived | NHIS A9; NASA App. F F.4.1 (test mode looks different). Total coding colours: 4 (A28 limit 6). | proposed |
| NHIS-ALERT-001 | IEC-style markers: Advisory `!`, Caution `!!`, Warning `!!!`, Emergency `!!!` plus its own label, glyph and ring. | nhis-derived | NHIS 01 states the IEC → NASA mapping is a design decision of that report. | proposed |
| NHIS-ALERT-002 | Alert glyph silhouettes: circle (Advisory), triangle (Caution), octagon (Warning), filled octagon inside a second ring (Emergency). | design-decision | NHIS A2, B5: priority must read without colour. | proposed |
| NHIS-ALERT-003 | Emergency renders as a critical overlay above every layer, double ring, asserting source shown first. An Emergency that fails validation is shown as a rejected integration error, never silently shown as another class. | nhis-derived | NHIS operational alert model; 03 §19 | proposed |
| NHIS-ALERT-004 | The Station alert list offers Inhibit for Advisory, Caution and Warning, not for an authorized Emergency. | design-decision | NASA App. F allows inhibit; NHIS keeps an externally asserted Emergency from being hidden by NASTRA UI. Needs project review: U-11. | conflict open |
| NHIS-ALERT-005 | Flashing: glyph only, 3 Hz at 50% duty, unacknowledged Warning and Emergency only, synchronised by wall clock, stops on acknowledge or suppress. Caution is steady. With reduced motion, flashing becomes a steady white ring. | nhis-derived | NHIS A27, B28; WCAG 2.3.1. Reduced-motion replacement needs review: U-10. | proposed |
| NHIS-DATA-001 | Data quality is achromatic: label, glyph, dashed outline, hatching and age. Stale values stay at full contrast. Freshness thresholds must be supplied per stream with an owner and rationale. | nhis-derived | NHIS A7, B33; NASA-03 usage boundary (no universal timeout); 03 §20 | proposed |
| NHIS-AI-001 | AI responses use distinct anatomy per evidence category: plain observation block, dashed bracketed assessment block, solid action block linked to its protocol. | nhis-derived | 02 §7; NHIS A11; 03 §18 | proposed |
| NHIS-AI-002 | High-consequence assessments can require the crew member's own report before the AI assessment is revealed (`requireSelfReport`). | nhis-derived | NHIS A14 (D) | proposed |
| NHIS-AI-003 | Persistent disclosure text and default confidence wording are placeholders for medical review. | design-decision | NHIS A16; effect of wording on reliance is unstudied (01). U-12. | not evaluated |
| NHIS-AI-004 | Values read from images are unconfirmed until the crew member checks them (`ExtractionReview`). | nhis-derived | Project rule (NASTRA CLAUDE.md "never write medical data from image extraction without confirmation"); NISTIR 7804 data accuracy via 01 | proposed |
| NHIS-VIZ-001 | Charts are custom SVG, not Carbon Charts. | design-decision | 03 §11.1 makes Carbon optional; custom SVG gives exact control of baseline, live/historical, gap and limit semantics with no extra dependency (03 §24). | proposed |
| NHIS-VIZ-002 | Default line-break threshold is three times the median sample spacing; streams can override. | design-decision | Prevents hourly streams from rendering as dots; not a freshness rule. | proposed |
| NHIS-VIZ-003 | Signed difference is against the baseline reference value when one is supplied, otherwise against the nearest band edge ("within baseline" inside the band). | design-decision | NHIS A24 requires a signed difference; NHIS does not invent a reference value. | proposed |
| NHIS-IMPL-002 | Alert list uses a native HTML table with React Aria buttons inside, not the React Aria Table grid. | design-decision | Native semantics and a single tab order are more predictable for a short audit list than a second grid keyboard model. | proposed |
| NHIS-IMPL-003 | NHIS draws its own glyph set instead of adopting an icon library. | design-decision | 03 §8 requires safety-significant icons to be reviewed and consistent; 24 px grid, 2 px stroke. | proposed |
| NHIS-LAYOUT-001 | Minimum Station viewport 1280 × 720. Overview = header, state line, active alert, status-bearing vitals, current AI assessment. Secondary readouts are one step away. | nhis-derived | NHIS A26; 02 §3.3 | proposed |
| NHIS-EVA-001 | Simulated display: 37° diagonal, 16:9, centred on the line of sight. | design-decision | Candidate displays 36–38° (Mitra et al. 2023 via 01). Joint AR places its display right of and above the forward view; that variant is not simulated (U-07). | not evaluated |
| NHIS-EVA-002 | With a 10° clear zone, persistent content lives only in two lateral bands. Slots: voice and periphery alerts upper, AI response and steps middle, status and consumables lower. | nhis-derived | NHIS B1, B9, B25 | not evaluated |
| NHIS-EVA-003 | EVA text: 0.7° for the glanced primary value, 0.5° for everything else. The 0.35° floor is not used by default. | nhis-derived | NHIS B20 | not evaluated |
| NHIS-EVA-004 | Warning and Emergency cue: an outline ring at the clear-zone edge plus a label chip in the right band within ±15° of the line of sight. Red only as the chip fill behind white text. | nhis-derived | NHIS B6, B22 | not evaluated |
| NHIS-EVA-005 | Lowlight opacity 0.55 for nominal items and non-current steps. | design-decision | NHIS B14 requires lowlighting, gives no value. Needs optical test. | not evaluated |
